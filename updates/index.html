Important dates, expected payments and new rules for the schemes on Kramavriddhi. We check official sources regularly and update this page.

📅 Expected14 Oct 2026

Annadata Sukhibhava 2nd instalment expected around Dasara

News reports say AP will pay it with PM-KISAN's 24th instalment, and some mention 17 October. The date and amount are reported, not officially confirmed. Complete your eKYC at the Rythu Seva Kendram.
📅 Expected14 Oct 2026

Thalliki Vandanam: ₹13,000 for new Class 1 and Inter students before Dasara

The 2nd phase covers 3,29,201 students: new Class 1 and Inter first-year admissions and those left out in July. The sanction order was issued on 10 October; no exact payment date yet.
⏰ Deadline13 Oct 2026

Central Sector Scholarship: apply by 31 October

College students in the top 20% of their Class 12 board with family income up to ₹4.5 lakh can get ₹12,000–₹20,000 a year. Apply on the National Scholarship Portal.
⏰ Deadline8 Oct 2026

Pragati Scholarship for girls: apply by 31 October

Girls in AICTE degree and diploma courses can get ₹50,000 a year. 2026-27 applications on the National Scholarship Portal close on 31 October 2026.
📅 Expected14 Oct 2026

PM-KISAN 24th instalment: no official date yet

Expected in October–November. Dates like 17 or 20 October in news and on social media are not confirmed by the government. Make sure your eKYC, land seeding and Aadhaar bank link are done.
📰 News14 Oct 2026

AP Auto Drivers Sevalo: 2026 payment date not announced

Last year auto, taxi and maxi-cab owner-drivers got ₹15,000 on 4 October 2025. No official date has been given for this year's payment yet.
⏰ Deadline8 Oct 2026

Deepam-2: book your free gas cylinder by 30 November

AP families are in the August–November period. An unused free cylinder does not carry over to the next period.
📰 News1 Oct 2026

Telangana: senior citizens' pension still ₹2,016

The promised increase to ₹4,000 has not been implemented for senior citizens, and they were not part of the 2026 Cheyutha enrolment round.
📈 Rate1 Oct 2026

Sukanya Samriddhi interest stays at 8.2% for Oct–Dec 2026

The rate is reviewed every quarter. The next announcement is expected at the end of December.
📰 News31 Jul 2026

PM-KISAN continued until 2030-31

The Union Cabinet approved continuing PM-KISAN from 2026-27 to 2030-31 with ₹3.15 lakh crore.
📰 News8 May 2026

Telangana: interest-free SHG loans raised to ₹10 lakh

Under Indira Mahila Shakti, the government now pays interest on SHG bank loans up to ₹10 lakh (earlier ₹5 lakh).
📰 News22 Apr 2026

PM Internship: final-year students can now apply

Final-year UG and PG students aged 18–25 can apply with an NOC from their college. Interns get at least ₹9,000 a month.
