Andhra Pradesh government schemes

Guides to Andhra Pradesh state schemes for farmers, pensioners, mothers and students, explained in simple language.

🚍

Stree Shakti Free Bus Travel (Andhra Pradesh)

Free travel for women, girls and transgender persons in APSRTC ordinary and express buses across AP.

Andhra PradeshWomen & Girls
🔥

Deepam-2 Free Gas Cylinders (Andhra Pradesh)

Three free LPG cylinders a year for rice card families, one every four months, refunded within 48 hours.

Andhra PradeshWomen & Girls
📚

Thalliki Vandanam (Andhra Pradesh)

₹15,000 a year for every school-going child in Classes 1–12, with ₹13,000 paid to the mother.

Andhra PradeshEducation & Skills
👵

NTR Bharosa Pension (Andhra Pradesh)

Monthly pensions of ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 for the elderly, widows, persons with disabilities and other groups.

Andhra PradeshPension & Insurance
🌾

Annadata Sukhibhava (Andhra Pradesh)

₹20,000 a year for AP farmer families: ₹14,000 from the state plus ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN, in three instalments.

Andhra PradeshFarmers & Agriculture
