Andhra Pradesh government schemes

Guides to Andhra Pradesh state schemes for farmers, pensioners, mothers and students, explained in simple language.

Nirudyoga Bhruthi Unemployment Allowance (Andhra Pradesh)

₹3,000 a month promised for unemployed youth in the 2024 Super Six. Not started as of October 2026: no order, no application.

Andhra PradeshJobs & Internships
👩

Aadabidda Nidhi ₹1,500 for Women (Andhra Pradesh)

₹1,500 a month promised for women aged 18+ in the 2024 Super Six. Not started as of October 2026: no order, no application.

Andhra PradeshWomen & Girls
🏥

Dr NTR Vaidya Seva Health Scheme (Andhra Pradesh)

Cashless treatment up to ₹25 lakh per family a year for 3,257 procedures. New universal health policy approved; start not yet confirmed.

Andhra PradeshHealth
🏫

Fee Reimbursement & Post-Matric Scholarships (Andhra Pradesh)

Full tuition fee for college students from families earning up to ₹2.5 lakh, plus ₹10,000–₹20,000 a year for food and hostel to the mother.

Andhra PradeshEducation & Skills
🐟

Matsyakarula Sevalo for Fishermen (Andhra Pradesh)

₹20,000 a year for sea fishermen families during the 15 April–14 June fishing ban. 2026 paid in May.

Andhra PradeshFarmers & Agriculture
🧵

Nethanna Sevalo for Handloom Weavers (Andhra Pradesh)

₹25,000 a year for BPL handloom weaver families who own a loom. 2nd phase payment date not yet announced.

Andhra PradeshBusiness & Self-employment
🛺

Auto Drivers Sevalo (Andhra Pradesh)

₹15,000 a year for auto, taxi and maxi-cab owner-drivers in AP. 2026 payment date not yet announced.

Andhra PradeshBusiness & Self-employment
🚍

Stree Shakti Free Bus Travel (Andhra Pradesh)

Free travel for women, girls and transgender persons in APSRTC ordinary and express buses across AP.

Andhra PradeshWomen & Girls
🔥

Deepam-2 Free Gas Cylinders (Andhra Pradesh)

Three free LPG cylinders a year for rice card families, one every four months, refunded within 48 hours.

Andhra PradeshWomen & Girls
📚

Thalliki Vandanam (Andhra Pradesh)

₹15,000 a year for every school-going child in Classes 1–12, with ₹13,000 paid to the mother.

Andhra PradeshEducation & Skills
👵

NTR Bharosa Pension (Andhra Pradesh)

Monthly pensions of ₹4,000 to ₹15,000 for the elderly, widows, persons with disabilities and other groups.

Andhra PradeshPension & Insurance
🌾

Annadata Sukhibhava (Andhra Pradesh)

₹20,000 a year for AP farmer families: ₹14,000 from the state plus ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN, in three instalments.

Andhra PradeshFarmers & Agriculture
