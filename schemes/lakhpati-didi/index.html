Lakhpati Didi (SHG women)

Lakhpati Didi helps women in rural self-help groups (SHGs) earn at least ₹1 lakh a year for their family, through low-cost loans, training and help to start or grow small businesses. Over 3 crore women had become Lakhpati Didis by May 2026.

What is Lakhpati Didi?

A Lakhpati Didi is a self-help group (SHG) member whose household earns ₹1 lakh or more a year on a sustained basis. The initiative, started in 2023, works through the government's rural livelihood mission, DAY-NRLM (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission). By May 2026, 3.07 crore rural women had become Lakhpati Didis.

What support do women get?

  • Revolving Fund of ₹20,000–₹30,000 per eligible SHG, to start lending within the group.
  • Community Investment Fund of up to ₹2.5 lakh per SHG, through village and cluster federations, for members' income activities.
  • Bank loans to SHGs at low interest, with interest subvention in many districts.
  • Training in business, money management and digital skills. A National Campaign on Entrepreneurship started in January 2026 to train 50 lakh SHG members.
  • Help to start livelihoods such as dairy, poultry, tailoring, food processing, shops, and services like Drone Didi (farm drones) and Bank Sakhi.

Who can join?

  • Women from poor rural households can join a self-help group of usually 10–20 women in their village.
  • SHG members who are active and want to start or grow an income activity get the Lakhpati Didi support.

How to join

  1. Ask your village's SHG leader, Village Organisation, or the Community Resource Person (CRP) to add you to an SHG.
  2. Attend weekly meetings, save small amounts regularly and keep records.
  3. After the SHG is graded, it can get the revolving fund and bank loans.
  4. Make a simple business plan with the CRP's help to use the loan for an income activity.

In Andhra Pradesh and Telangana

  • Andhra Pradesh: SHGs are supported by SERP (rural) and MEPMA (towns). Ask at your village/ward secretariat.
  • Telangana: SHGs are supported by SERP and MEPMA, and also get interest-free loans up to ₹10 lakh under Indira Mahila Shakti.

Frequently asked questions

Who is a Lakhpati Didi?

A self-help group member whose household earns ₹1 lakh or more a year on a sustained basis.

How many Lakhpati Didis are there?

Over 3.07 crore rural women had become Lakhpati Didis by May 2026.

Do I get ₹1 lakh directly from the government?

No. The scheme helps you earn ₹1 lakh a year through SHG loans, training and business support. It is not a direct cash payment.

How do I join a self-help group?

Contact your village SHG leader, Village Organisation or Community Resource Person (CRP), or ask at your village/ward secretariat.

What businesses can Lakhpati Didis do?

Dairy, poultry, tailoring, food processing, small shops, services such as drone spraying (Drone Didi) and banking help (Bank Sakhi), and more.
