Fee Reimbursement & Post-Matric Scholarships (Telangana)

In Telangana, SC, ST, BC, EBC, minority and disabled students in colleges can get their tuition fee reimbursed and a monthly maintenance allowance through the ePASS portal. From 2026-27, the government says it will pay the money directly into students' bank accounts.

What is fee reimbursement in Telangana?

Under the post-matric scholarship schemes, the Telangana government reimburses the tuition fee of eligible students in courses after Class 10 (Intermediate, degree, engineering, pharmacy, PG and others) and pays a maintenance allowance. Everything is handled on the ePASS portal of the welfare departments.

Latest update (2026-27)

  • Through G.O. No. 9 (June 2026), the government decided to credit the fee reimbursement directly to students' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts; students then pay their colleges.
  • Private colleges challenged parts of this order. In July 2026 the High Court said colleges must admit students for 2026-27 without asking for fees upfront, and may collect fees directly only if the government misses its payment deadlines. The case is still in court.
  • Colleges say about ₹2,800 crore of older reimbursement is still pending. There have been repeated college bandhs over these dues since 2024.

Who is eligible?

From the official ePASS portal:

CategoryAnnual family income
SC₹2,50,000 or less
ST₹2,00,000 or less
BC, EBC and Minority (village)₹1,50,000 or less
BC, EBC and Minority (town)₹2,00,000 or less
Disabled (DW)₹1,00,000 or less (parents' income)
  • For renewal: 75% attendance at the end of each quarter and promotion to the next year.

Who is not eligible?

  • Part-time, online and correspondence courses.
  • Sponsored seats, management quota seats and spot admissions.
  • Students already getting a stipend or scholarship under another scheme.
  • BC, EBC and disabled students in open university / distance courses or category-B seats in MBBS and BDS.
  • Students studying a course at the same level again.

How to apply

  1. Go to telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in and choose the post-matric scholarship fresh registration (or renewal) for your category.
  2. Fill in your personal, course and family income details and upload documents. Note your application ID.
  3. Complete biometric (Aadhaar) authentication at a MeeSeva centre if asked, and submit the printout and documents to your college.
  4. Your college and the welfare officer verify the application. Track it with "Know Your Application Status" on the portal.

Documents usually needed

  • Aadhaar (your name must match your SSC certificate exactly)
  • Income certificate and caste certificate from MeeSeva (a fresh income certificate for the year)
  • SSC / Intermediate marks memos and study (bonafide) certificates
  • Aadhaar-seeded bank account in your name
  • Admission or allotment order (for EAMCET, ECET and other CET seats)
Applying on ePASS is free. Keep your bank account Aadhaar-seeded, because from 2026-27 the money is meant to come to your account. If a college stops your admission or certificates over fees, contact the district welfare officer.
Official portal: telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in · Technical help: 040-23120311

Frequently asked questions

What is the income limit for fee reimbursement in Telangana?

₹2.5 lakh for SC, ₹2 lakh for ST, ₹1.5 lakh (village) or ₹2 lakh (town) for BC, EBC and minority students, and ₹1 lakh for disabled students, as per the ePASS portal.

Is fee reimbursement paid to students or colleges in Telangana from 2026-27?

The government's G.O. No. 9 (June 2026) says it will be credited to students' Aadhaar-linked bank accounts, and students pay their colleges. Parts of the order are being heard in the High Court.

Can colleges ask for fees upfront in 2026-27?

In July 2026 the High Court said colleges must admit students without demanding fees upfront, and can collect fees directly only if the government misses its deadlines.

Are management quota students eligible?

No. Sponsored seats, management quota seats and spot admissions are not eligible.

Where do I apply?

On telanganaepass.cgg.gov.in, then submit the printout and documents to your college for verification.
