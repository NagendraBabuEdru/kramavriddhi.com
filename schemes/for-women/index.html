Schemes for girls & women

Scholarships, savings, business loans and everyday support for girls and women, from the Central Government, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, all in one place.

🎓 Education

Help for girls to study, from school to technical degrees.

👧

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

A high-interest, tax-free savings account for a girl child under 10, for her education and marriage.

Women & Girls
👩‍🎓

CBSE Single Girl Child Scholarship

₹1,000 a month in Class 11–12 for an only daughter who scored 70%+ in CBSE Class 10.

Education & Skills
🎓

AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls

₹50,000 a year for girls in AICTE-approved degree and diploma courses. 2026-27 last date: 31 October.

Education & Skills
📚

Thalliki Vandanam (Andhra Pradesh)

₹15,000 a year for every school-going child in Classes 1–12, with ₹13,000 paid to the mother.

Andhra PradeshEducation & Skills

💼 Self-employment & business

Loans, training and support for women who want to earn.

💪

Lakhpati Didi (SHG women)

Loans, training and business help so rural self-help group women earn ₹1 lakh+ a year.

Women & Girls
🏪

Indira Mahila Shakti (Telangana)

Interest-free loans up to ₹10 lakh for SHGs, and women-run canteens, buses, petrol bunks and solar plants.

TelanganaBusiness & Self-employment
💼

PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Collateral-free business loans up to ₹20 lakh for small shops, services, manufacturing and allied farm activities.

Business & Self-employment
Being relaunched: Stand-Up India (bank loans of ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore for women and SC/ST entrepreneurs) ended in March 2025 and the government announced in March 2026 that a revamped version is being prepared. PMEGP (subsidy for new businesses, with a higher subsidy for women) ran until 31 March 2026, and its extension is under consideration. We will add full guides when the new rules are announced.
