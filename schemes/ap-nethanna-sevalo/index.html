Nethanna Sevalo for Handloom Weavers (Andhra Pradesh)

Nethanna Sevalo (also written Nethannala Sevalo) is the Andhra Pradesh government's support for handloom weavers. The head of every eligible weaver family that owns a handloom gets ₹25,000 a year, paid directly into the bank account.

What is Nethanna Sevalo?

The Andhra Pradesh government started Nethanna Sevalo on 7 August 2026 (National Handloom Day) at Chirala in Bapatla district. It gives ₹25,000 a year to each eligible handloom weaver family, up from ₹24,000 under the earlier Nethanna Nestham scheme. The rules are in G.O.Ms.No.187 (29 July 2026) of the Industries and Commerce (Handlooms and Textiles) Department.

Latest update

  • 1st phase: on 7 August 2026 the government released about ₹179 crore for 71,536 handloom families.
  • 2nd phase: 36,632 applications were received from weavers left out of the first list. Handloom officials are checking them with home visits, and the deadline for finalising the list was extended to 3 October 2026.
2nd phase payment: date not announced (15 October 2026). The Handlooms Minister has said it will be paid "shortly" to all eligible weavers. We will update this page when the money is released.

Who is eligible?

According to G.O.Ms.No.187:

  • The money goes to the head of the weaver family who owns a handloom, whatever their age.
  • One benefit per family, however many looms the family owns.
  • The family must be below the poverty line (BPL), usually a rice card family.
  • The family must actually weave on the handloom for a living and be in the Handlooms Department's database.
  • You must live in Andhra Pradesh.

How are weavers selected?

  1. The Handlooms and Textiles Department prepares the list from its weaver database, re-verified through the village and ward secretariats.
  2. The list is displayed at your Swarna Grama / Swarna Ward secretariat for a social audit, so you can check your name.
  3. If your name is missing or wrong, ask at the secretariat or the Assistant Director (Handlooms and Textiles) office. Officials may visit your home to see your loom.
  4. After the District Collector approves the final list, ₹25,000 is credited by DBT to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.

Keep these ready

  • Aadhaar card and rice card
  • Bank account linked with Aadhaar (and NPCI-mapped for DBT)
  • Your loom: officials check that it is a working handloom in your house
No one needs to pay any fee or "commission" to be added to the list. Report anyone asking for money to your secretariat.

Other help for weavers

  • Free power: 200 units a month for handloom families and 500 units for power loom units, announced at the launch.
  • Pension: weavers can also get the ₹4,000 monthly NTR Bharosa pension (age and other rules apply).
  • Loans and tools: see PM Vishwakarma and PM Mudra Yojana.
Help: your village or ward secretariat, or the Assistant Director (Handlooms and Textiles) office in your district
Official state portal: ap.gov.in

Frequently asked questions

How much do weavers get under Nethanna Sevalo?

₹25,000 a year for each eligible handloom weaver family, paid to the head of the family by DBT.

Who is eligible for Nethanna Sevalo?

BPL handloom weaver families in Andhra Pradesh who own a handloom, actually weave for a living and are in the Handlooms Department database. There is no age limit.

Do families with more than one loom get more money?

No. Each weaver family gets only one benefit, however many looms it owns.

When will the 2nd phase of Nethanna Sevalo be paid?

No date has been announced as of 15 October 2026. 36,632 applications were received and the list was to be finalised by 3 October.

Is Nethanna Sevalo for power loom weavers?

The ₹25,000 is for handloom weavers who own a handloom. Power loom units get separate free power support.
