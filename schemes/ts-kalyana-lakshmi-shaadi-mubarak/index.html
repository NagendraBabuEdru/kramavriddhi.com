Kalyana Lakshmi & Shaadi Mubarak (Telangana)

Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak are Telangana government schemes that give ₹1,00,116 once for the marriage of a girl from a poor family. Kalyana Lakshmi is for SC, ST, BC and EBC families, and Shaadi Mubarak is for minority families. The money is paid to the bride's mother's bank account.

What are Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak?

Telangana started these schemes on 2 October 2014 so that poor families don't fall into debt for a daughter's wedding. Both give the same amount, ₹1,00,116, once per bride:

  • Kalyana Lakshmi: for brides from SC, ST, BC and EBC families.
  • Shaadi Mubarak: for brides from minority families.

As per the latest government order, the money is credited to the bride's mother's bank account.

Latest update (2026)

  • In August 2026, a single judge of the Telangana High Court put the schemes on hold on a petition questioning them. On 1 September 2026, a Division Bench headed by the Chief Justice stayed that order, so the schemes continue.
  • MLAs were still handing over Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak cheques in October 2026, but many families report long waits. Opposition leaders say about two lakh applications are pending; the government has not given an official figure.
About "1 tola gold": before the 2023 elections, Congress promised one tola of gold to brides along with Kalyana Lakshmi. As of October 2026, this has not been implemented, and there is no government order for it. Don't pay anyone who offers to "register" you for the gold.

Who is eligible?

  • The bride's family must live in Telangana.
  • The bride must be 18 or older on the wedding day (the groom 21 or older).
  • It is for the bride's first marriage, and each bride can get it only once.
  • Family income limits, from the official ePASS portal:

How to apply

  1. Open the official Telangana ePASS portal and choose Kalyana Lakshmi / Shaadi Mubarak.
  2. Click Registration under your scheme, fill in the bride's, mother's and marriage details, and upload the documents.
  3. Enter the bride's mother's bank account details correctly (Aadhaar-linked). If you applied earlier, use the Edit/Uploads link to update them.
  4. Print the application and submit it with the original documents as instructed, usually at your Tahsildar (MRO) office.
  5. Revenue officials verify the details (sometimes with a home visit), and the RDO sanctions the amount.
  6. Check progress any time with Print/Status on the ePASS page.

Apply soon after the wedding. Late applications are often rejected; ask your Tahsildar office about the current time limit.

Documents usually needed

  • Aadhaar of the bride, her mother and the groom
  • Caste certificate (Kalyana Lakshmi) or minority proof (Shaadi Mubarak)
  • Income certificate from MeeSeva, within the limit and not expired
  • Bride's age proof: SSC certificate or birth certificate
  • Marriage proof: wedding card and photo, and a marriage certificate or certificate from the VRO / Panchayat Secretary
  • Bank passbook of the bride's mother, linked with Aadhaar

Frequently asked questions

How much is Kalyana Lakshmi in Telangana?

₹1,00,116, given once for the marriage of an eligible bride. Shaadi Mubarak gives the same amount for minority brides.

What is the income limit for Kalyana Lakshmi?

₹2 lakh a year for SC and ST families and for BC/EBC families in towns; ₹1.5 lakh for BC/EBC families in villages. Shaadi Mubarak's limit is ₹2 lakh.

Whose account gets the Kalyana Lakshmi money?

As per the latest government order, the amount is credited to the bride's mother's bank account.

Is the 1 tola gold given with Kalyana Lakshmi?

No. The promise of one tola gold has not been implemented as of October 2026, and there is no government order for it.

Are Kalyana Lakshmi and Shaadi Mubarak stopped by the High Court?

No. A single judge's order in August 2026 put them on hold, but the Division Bench stayed that order on 1 September 2026, so the schemes continue.
