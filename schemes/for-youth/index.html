Opportunities for youth

Paid internships, defence jobs, free skill training and help to start your own work: government opportunities for young people, all in one place.

🧑‍💼 Jobs & internships

Earn while you learn, or serve the country.

🧑‍💼

PM Internship Scheme

Paid internships in top companies for ages 18–25: at least ₹9,000 a month plus ₹6,000 one-time.

Jobs & Internships
🎖️

Agnipath Scheme (Agniveer)

Four years in the Army, Navy or Air Force: ₹30,000–₹40,000 a month and about ₹11.71 lakh tax-free at exit.

Jobs & Internships

🛠️ Skills & education

Free training and scholarships to build your career.

🛠️

PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

Free short skill-training courses with a government certificate, for anyone aged 15 to 59.

Education & Skills
🎓

AICTE Pragati Scholarship for Girls

₹50,000 a year for girls in AICTE-approved degree and diploma courses. 2026-27 last date: 31 October.

Education & Skills

💼 Start your own work

Loans and support for your own business or trade.

💼

PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Collateral-free business loans up to ₹20 lakh for small shops, services, manufacturing and allied farm activities.

Business & Self-employment
🔨

PM Vishwakarma

For carpenters, tailors, barbers and other artisans: paid training, ₹15,000 toolkit and loans up to ₹3 lakh at 5%.

Business & Self-employment
🚀

Rajiv Yuva Vikasam (Telangana)

Announced ₹50,000–₹4 lakh self-employment support for SC/ST/BC/minority youth. Not yet implemented (2026).

TelanganaBusiness & Self-employment
Being relaunched: Stand-Up India and PMEGP (subsidised loans for new businesses) are being renewed by the government. We will add guides when the new rules are announced. See schemes for girls & women →

🛡️ Secure your future

Low-cost savings, pension and insurance to start early.

🏦

PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

A zero-balance bank account with a free RuPay card, ₹2 lakh accident cover and an overdraft of up to ₹10,000.

Banking
👴

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

A guaranteed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month after 60, for anyone aged 18–40 who is not an income-tax payer.

Pension & Insurance
🛡️

PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

₹2 lakh life insurance for ₹436 a year, for anyone aged 18–50 with a bank or post office account.

Pension & Insurance
🩹

PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh for just ₹20 a year, for anyone aged 18–70 with a bank account.

Pension & Insurance
