Schemes for boys & men

Most government schemes are open to everyone. This page brings together the ones most useful for boys and men at each stage of life: studies, first job, work, business and family.

🎓 Studies & skills

Scholarships and free training for students and young people.

🎓

Central Sector Scholarship for College Students (CSSS)

₹12,000–₹20,000 a year for college students in the top 20% of their Class 12 board. 2026-27 last date: 31 October.

Education & Skills
🛠️

PM Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

Free short skill-training courses with a government certificate, for anyone aged 15 to 59.

Education & Skills
🧑‍💼

PM Internship Scheme

Paid internships in top companies for ages 18–25: at least ₹9,000 a month plus ₹6,000 one-time.

Jobs & Internships

💼 First job & career

Paid internships, first-job rewards and defence careers.

🧾

PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (First Job)

Up to ₹15,000 for first-time employees who join a job with PF (EPFO) between Aug 2025 and Jul 2027.

Jobs & Internships
🎖️

Agnipath Scheme (Agniveer)

Four years in the Army, Navy or Air Force: ₹30,000–₹40,000 a month and about ₹11.71 lakh tax-free at exit.

Jobs & Internships

👷 Workers & labourers

Registration, pension and insurance for daily-wage and unorganised workers.

👷

e-Shram Card for Unorganised Workers

Free national ID for daily-wage, gig and other unorganised workers aged 16–59, linking them to welfare schemes.

Jobs & Internships
🧓

PM Shram Yogi Maandhan (Pension for Workers)

₹3,000 monthly pension at 60 for workers earning up to ₹15,000 a month. Join at 18–40; the government matches your payment.

Pension & Insurance
👴

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

A guaranteed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month after 60, for anyone aged 18–40 who is not an income-tax payer.

Pension & Insurance
🩹

PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh for just ₹20 a year, for anyone aged 18–70 with a bank account.

Pension & Insurance
🛡️

PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

₹2 lakh life insurance for ₹436 a year, for anyone aged 18–50 with a bank or post office account.

Pension & Insurance

🛒 Own business, trades & drivers

Loans and support for vendors, artisans, weavers, fishermen, drivers and small business owners.

🛒

PM SVANidhi (Loans for Street Vendors)

Collateral-free loans of ₹15,000, ₹25,000 and ₹50,000 for street vendors, with a 7% interest subsidy. Open until 2030.

Business & Self-employment
💼

PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Collateral-free business loans up to ₹20 lakh for small shops, services, manufacturing and allied farm activities.

Business & Self-employment
🔨

PM Vishwakarma

For carpenters, tailors, barbers and other artisans: paid training, ₹15,000 toolkit and loans up to ₹3 lakh at 5%.

Business & Self-employment
🧵

Nethanna Sevalo for Handloom Weavers (Andhra Pradesh)

₹25,000 a year for BPL handloom weaver families who own a loom. 2nd phase payment date not yet announced.

Andhra PradeshBusiness & Self-employment
🐟

Matsyakarula Sevalo for Fishermen (Andhra Pradesh)

₹20,000 a year for sea fishermen families during the 15 April–14 June fishing ban. 2026 paid in May.

Andhra PradeshFarmers & Agriculture
🛺

Auto Drivers Sevalo (Andhra Pradesh)

₹15,000 a year for auto, taxi and maxi-cab owner-drivers in AP. 2026 payment date not yet announced.

Andhra PradeshBusiness & Self-employment
🚀

Rajiv Yuva Vikasam (Telangana)

Announced ₹50,000–₹4 lakh self-employment support for SC/ST/BC/minority youth. First phase planned from 19 Nov 2026.

TelanganaBusiness & Self-employment

🌾 Farmers

Income support and crop insurance for farmer families.

🌾

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

₹6,000 a year for land-holding farmer families, paid in three ₹2,000 instalments straight to the bank account.

Farmers & Agriculture
🌦️

PM Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

Low-cost crop insurance against drought, floods, pests and unseasonal rain. AP Rabi 2026-27: enrol by 15 Dec (paddy 31 Dec).

Farmers & Agriculture
🌾

Annadata Sukhibhava (Andhra Pradesh)

₹20,000 a year for AP farmer families: ₹14,000 from the state plus ₹6,000 from PM-KISAN, in three instalments.

Andhra PradeshFarmers & Agriculture
🚜

Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

₹12,000 per acre every year for Telangana farmers, paid as ₹6,000 per acre each crop season.

TelanganaFarmers & Agriculture

🏥 Health & family

Free treatment and savings for your family.

🏥

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

Free hospital treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year, plus cover for every citizen aged 70 and above.

Health
🩺

Rajiv Aarogyasri (Telangana)

Cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh per family per year for 1,835 procedures, for white ration card families.

TelanganaHealth
👧

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

A high-interest, tax-free savings account for a girl child under 10, for her education and marriage.
