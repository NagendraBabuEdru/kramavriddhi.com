PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (Rooftop Solar)

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana helps families put solar panels on their own roof. The Central Government gives a subsidy of up to ₹78,000, so a home can get up to 300 units of electricity a month from the sun and cut its power bill.

What is PM Surya Ghar?

The Union Cabinet approved PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana on 29 February 2024 with ₹75,021 crore, to put rooftop solar on 1 crore homes by March 2027. It is run by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) with your electricity distribution company (DISCOM). By July 2026 more than 50 lakh homes had installed rooftop solar under it.

How much subsidy?

System sizeCentral subsidy
1 kW₹30,000
2 kW₹60,000
3 kW or more₹78,000 (maximum)

That is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW. Housing societies / RWAs get ₹18,000 per kW for common facilities. Some states add their own top-up. A 1–2 kW system usually suits homes using up to 150 units a month; 2–3 kW suits 150–300 units.

Who is eligible?

  • An Indian household that owns a house with a roof suitable for solar panels.
  • A valid electricity connection in the household's name.
  • The household should not have taken any other subsidy for solar panels.

How to apply

  1. Register on pmsuryaghar.gov.in (or the PM Surya Ghar app) with your state, DISCOM, electricity consumer number and mobile number.
  2. Apply for rooftop solar and get feasibility approval from your DISCOM.
  3. Get the system installed by a registered vendor listed on the portal.
  4. Apply for net metering. After the DISCOM inspects and commissions it, submit your bank details and the commissioning report on the portal.
  5. The subsidy is credited to your bank account.

Loans: collateral-free loans at low interest (around 7%) are available from public sector banks for systems up to 3 kW. Apply through the portal or your bank.

Applying is free. Use only vendors registered on the portal, and don't pay anyone who promises "free solar" in exchange for your electricity bill or Aadhaar details.
Official portal: pmsuryaghar.gov.in · Helpline: 15555
Help: your DISCOM (APSPDCL, APEPDCL, APCPDCL in AP; TGSPDCL, TGNPDCL in Telangana)

Frequently asked questions

How much subsidy does PM Surya Ghar give?

₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 (maximum) for 3 kW or more.

Is electricity really free under PM Surya Ghar?

The rooftop system can produce up to 300 units a month for a typical home, which can cut your bill to near zero depending on your use.

Who can apply for PM Surya Ghar?

Households that own a house with a suitable roof and a valid electricity connection, and haven't taken another solar subsidy.

Can I get a loan for rooftop solar?

Yes. Public sector banks give collateral-free loans at low interest for systems up to 3 kW.

Till when is PM Surya Ghar open?

The scheme runs till FY 2026-27, that is March 2027.
