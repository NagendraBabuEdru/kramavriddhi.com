Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

Rythu Bharosa is the Telangana government's investment support for farmers. Farmers get ₹12,000 per acre every year, paid as ₹6,000 per acre for each crop season, directly into their bank accounts.

What is Rythu Bharosa?

Rythu Bharosa replaced the earlier Rythu Bandhu scheme. It gives farmers money for each crop season to meet cultivation costs like seeds, fertiliser and labour, without needing a loan.

Latest update

The Telangana government released Rythu Bharosa funds for the 2026 Kharif season from 30 June 2026. A ₹500 per quintal bonus for notified fine rice varieties was also approved for the Kharif season.

Benefits

  • ₹12,000 per acre per year.
  • Paid in two parts: ₹6,000 per acre for Kharif (monsoon) and ₹6,000 per acre for Rabi (winter).
  • Money goes directly to the farmer's bank account (DBT).

Who is eligible?

  • Farmers in Telangana whose cultivable land is recorded in the state's land records (Bhu Bharati).
  • Payment is for land that is actually suitable for farming.

Land that is excluded

  • Non-agricultural land such as real-estate layouts, industrial areas and mining land.
  • Land that is not cultivable, and lands the government has excluded after verification.

What you need

  • Land recorded in your name (pattadar passbook)
  • Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Correct details with your Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO)

How to get it or fix problems

  1. Eligible farmers are identified from land records, so you usually don't need to apply each season.
  2. If money hasn't come, contact your Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) or Mandal Agriculture Officer with your passbook, Aadhaar and bank details.
  3. New land owners (after purchase or inheritance) should get their records updated in Bhu Bharati and inform the AEO.
Official portal: rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in
Help: Agriculture Extension Officer (AEO) or Mandal Agriculture Office

Frequently asked questions

How much is Rythu Bharosa per acre?

₹12,000 per acre per year, paid as ₹6,000 per acre for each of the Kharif and Rabi seasons.

When was Rythu Bharosa released for Kharif 2026?

The Telangana government began releasing Kharif 2026 Rythu Bharosa funds from 30 June 2026.

Is Rythu Bharosa paid for non-agricultural land?

No. Real-estate layouts, industrial and mining lands and non-cultivable land are excluded.

Do I need to apply every season for Rythu Bharosa?

Usually not. Farmers are identified from land records. If payment is missing, contact your Agriculture Extension Officer.

Is Rythu Bharosa the same as Rythu Bandhu?

Rythu Bharosa replaced Rythu Bandhu, with a higher amount and changes in which lands qualify.
