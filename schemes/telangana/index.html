Telangana government schemes

Guides to Telangana state schemes for farmers, women and families, explained in simple language.

🚀

Rajiv Yuva Vikasam (Telangana)

Announced ₹50,000–₹4 lakh self-employment support for SC/ST/BC/minority youth. Not yet implemented (2026).

TelanganaBusiness & Self-employment
🏪

Indira Mahila Shakti (Telangana)

Interest-free loans up to ₹10 lakh for SHGs, and women-run canteens, buses, petrol bunks and solar plants.

TelanganaBusiness & Self-employment
👵

Cheyutha Pension (Telangana)

Monthly pensions for poor elderly people, widows, single women, persons with disabilities and other groups.

TelanganaPension & Insurance
🩺

Rajiv Aarogyasri (Telangana)

Cashless treatment up to ₹10 lakh per family per year for 1,835 procedures, for white ration card families.

TelanganaHealth
💡

Gruha Jyothi Free Electricity (Telangana)

Zero electricity bill for eligible households that use up to 200 units a month.

TelanganaHousing
🏘️

Indiramma Indlu (Telangana)

Up to ₹5 lakh to build a pucca house for poor families who don't own one, paid in stages.

TelanganaHousing
🚌

Mahalakshmi Scheme (Telangana)

Free TGSRTC bus travel for women and girls, and ₹500 LPG cylinders for eligible families.

TelanganaWomen & Girls
🚜

Rythu Bharosa (Telangana)

₹12,000 per acre every year for Telangana farmers, paid as ₹6,000 per acre each crop season.

TelanganaFarmers & Agriculture
