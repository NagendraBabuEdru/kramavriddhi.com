Government schemes

Central schemes, explained simply

Clear guides to Government of India schemes: who can apply, what you get, which documents you need, and how to apply on the official portal.

🩹

PM Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY)

Accident insurance up to ₹2 lakh for just ₹20 a year, for anyone aged 18–70 with a bank account.

Pension & Insurance
🛡️

PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

₹2 lakh life insurance for ₹436 a year, for anyone aged 18–50 with a bank or post office account.

Pension & Insurance
🏦

PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)

A zero-balance bank account with a free RuPay card, ₹2 lakh accident cover and an overdraft of up to ₹10,000.

Banking
👴

Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

A guaranteed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month after 60, for anyone aged 18–40 who is not an income-tax payer.

Pension & Insurance
💼

PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

Collateral-free business loans up to ₹20 lakh for small shops, services, manufacturing and allied farm activities.

Business & Self-employment
🏡

PM Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

₹1.2 lakh (₹1.3 lakh in hilly and North-East areas) to build a pucca house in villages, plus toilet and wage support.

Housing
🏠

PM Awas Yojana – Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)

Help to build, buy or rent a house in towns and cities: ₹2.5 lakh assistance or up to ₹1.8 lakh home-loan subsidy.

Housing
👧

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

A high-interest, tax-free savings account for a girl child under 10, for her education and marriage.

Women & Girls
🏥

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

Free hospital treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family per year, plus cover for every citizen aged 70 and above.

Health
🌾

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

₹6,000 a year for land-holding farmer families, paid in three ₹2,000 instalments straight to the bank account.

Farmers & Agriculture
