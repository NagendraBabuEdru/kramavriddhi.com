PM Shram Yogi Maandhan (Pension for Workers)

PM Shram Yogi Maandhan is a pension scheme for unorganised workers earning up to ₹15,000 a month. You pay a small amount every month from age 18–40, the government adds the same amount, and you get a guaranteed pension of ₹3,000 a month after 60.

What is PM-SYM?

Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan (PM-SYM) is a voluntary, contributory pension scheme of the Ministry of Labour & Employment, started in February 2019. Over 54 lakh workers had enrolled by July 2026.

Benefits

  • A guaranteed pension of ₹3,000 per month from age 60.
  • The Central Government matches your contribution rupee for rupee.
  • If the pensioner dies, the spouse gets 50% of the pension as family pension.

Who can join?

  • Unorganised workers aged 18 to 40 (for example daily-wage workers, drivers, vendors, domestic workers, construction and farm workers).
  • Monthly income ₹15,000 or less.
  • Not covered by EPF, ESIC or NPS, not an income-tax payer, and not getting another government pension.

How much do you pay?

You pay the same amount every month until 60. Some examples from the official chart:

Payment is usually by monthly auto-debit from your savings or Jan Dhan account. If you miss payments, you can bring the account up to date by paying the dues with a small penalty.

Documents needed

  • Aadhaar card
  • Savings bank or Jan Dhan account (with IFSC)
  • Mobile number

How to join

  1. Visit your nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) with Aadhaar and bank details, or enrol yourself on maandhan.in.
  2. Pay the first contribution in cash at the CSC; later payments are auto-debited.
  3. You get a Shram Yogi card with your pension account number.

The portal also shows your account statement and claim status. Being registered on e-Shram helps too.

Official portal: maandhan.in (Ministry of Labour & Employment)

Frequently asked questions

How much pension does PM Shram Yogi Maandhan give?

₹3,000 a month from age 60, with 50% family pension for the spouse.

How much do I have to pay?

₹55 a month if you join at 18, up to ₹200 a month if you join at 40. The government adds the same amount.

Who can join PM-SYM?

Unorganised workers aged 18–40 with monthly income up to ₹15,000, who are not EPF/ESIC/NPS members or income-tax payers.

Where do I join?

At a Common Service Centre with Aadhaar and a savings or Jan Dhan account, or on maandhan.in.

Is PM-SYM the same as Atal Pension Yojana?

No. PM-SYM is for unorganised workers earning up to ₹15,000 and gives a fixed ₹3,000; APY lets you choose ₹1,000–₹5,000 and is open to anyone 18–40 who is not an income-tax payer.
