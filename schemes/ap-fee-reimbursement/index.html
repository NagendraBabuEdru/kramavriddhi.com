Fee Reimbursement & Post-Matric Scholarships (Andhra Pradesh)

In Andhra Pradesh, college students from poorer families can get their full tuition fee paid by the government (RTF) and a yearly maintenance grant for food and hostel costs (MTF). Applications go through the Jnanabhumi portal with the help of your college.

What is fee reimbursement in AP?

Andhra Pradesh runs two kinds of post-matric support for students after Class 10, in ITI, polytechnic, degree, engineering, medicine, PG and similar courses:

  • RTF (Reimbursement of Tuition Fee): full fee reimbursement, covering tuition, special fees, other fees and exam fees fixed by the competent authority.
  • MTF (Maintenance): a yearly grant for food and hostel costs: ₹10,000 for ITI, ₹15,000 for polytechnic and ₹20,000 for degree and higher courses. It is credited to the mother's bank account.

Earlier these were called Jagananna Vidya Deevena and Vasathi Deevena. Applications and payments are handled through the Jnanabhumi portal.

Latest update

  • On 10 February 2026 the state released about ₹1,200 crore for fee reimbursement and scholarships of the 2024-25 academic year (₹736 crore for BC students, ₹365 crore for EWS and ₹99 crore for minority students).
  • The present government pays the tuition fee to the college managements instead of to students' mothers, as the earlier government did.
  • Dates for 2026-27 applications are announced through colleges and the Jnanabhumi portal. Ask your college's scholarship section.

Who is eligible?

According to the government order and the Jnanabhumi portal, for SC, ST, BC, EBC/EWS, Kapu, minority and differently-abled students:

  • Total family income ₹2.5 lakh a year or less.
  • Family land below 10 acres of wet land, or 25 acres of dry land, or 25 acres of wet and dry together.
  • No family member is a government employee or pensioner (sanitary workers are eligible) or an income-tax payer.
  • No family member owns a four-wheeler (taxi, tractor and auto are allowed).
  • In towns, the family owns no property or less than 1,500 sq ft of built-up area.
  • At least 75% attendance is needed for the money to be released.

How to apply

  1. After admission, give your details to your college's scholarship section. The college registers eligible students on jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in.
  2. Keep ready: Aadhaar, rice card / income certificate, caste certificate, previous marks memos, the mother's Aadhaar-linked bank account, and admission details.
  3. Your details are verified by the welfare department and the village/ward secretariat (household data).
  4. Check your status on the Jnanabhumi portal or at your secretariat.
There is no fee for fee reimbursement. Never pay anyone to "get your fee reimbursement sanctioned." If your college refuses admission or certificates because of pending reimbursement, complain to the welfare department or the district collector.
Official portal: jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in
Help: your college scholarship section, the district Social / BC / Minority welfare office, or your village/ward secretariat

Frequently asked questions

What is the income limit for fee reimbursement in AP?

Total family income of ₹2.5 lakh a year or less, plus land and other conditions.

How much is the MTF (maintenance) amount in AP?

₹10,000 a year for ITI, ₹15,000 for polytechnic and ₹20,000 for degree and higher courses, credited to the mother's account.

Is fee reimbursement paid to the college or to the mother?

The present government pays the tuition fee (RTF) to the college. The maintenance grant (MTF) goes to the mother's account.

When was AP fee reimbursement last released?

On 10 February 2026, about ₹1,200 crore was released for the 2024-25 academic year.

Where do I apply for AP fee reimbursement?

Through your college, which registers eligible students on the Jnanabhumi portal (jnanabhumi.ap.gov.in).
