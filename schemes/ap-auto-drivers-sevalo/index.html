Auto Drivers Sevalo (Andhra Pradesh)

Auto Drivers Sevalo is an Andhra Pradesh scheme that gives ₹15,000 a year to auto-rickshaw, taxi (motor cab) and maxi-cab drivers who own and drive their own vehicle. It was started in October 2025 to support drivers after free bus travel for women began.

What is Auto Drivers Sevalo?

The Andhra Pradesh government launched Auto Drivers Sevalo on 4 October 2025 in Vijayawada. About 2.9 lakh drivers received ₹15,000 each (about ₹436 crore in total) directly in their bank accounts. The government said the scheme supports drivers whose income was affected by Stree Shakti free bus travel for women.

2026-27 status: news reports say funds have been earmarked for Auto Drivers Sevalo in 2026-27, but as of 13 October 2026 we have not found an official date for the second year's payment. We will update this page when it is announced.

Who is eligible?

  • Drivers of auto-rickshaws, motor cabs (taxis) and maxi cabs.
  • You must own the vehicle and drive it yourself.
  • A valid driving licence and vehicle registration in Andhra Pradesh.
  • Other welfare conditions set by the government apply (for example household and income checks done by the secretariat).

Documents usually needed

  • Driving licence
  • Vehicle RC (registration certificate) in your name
  • Aadhaar and an Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Rice card / household details, if asked

Missed the payment?

Eligible drivers who did not get the money were told they could apply, and the government would check their eligibility. Contact your village or ward secretariat with your licence, RC and Aadhaar.

Help: your village/ward secretariat or the district transport office
Official state portal: ap.gov.in

Frequently asked questions

How much do auto drivers get in Andhra Pradesh?

₹15,000 a year under Auto Drivers Sevalo, paid directly to the bank account.

Who is eligible for Auto Drivers Sevalo?

Auto, taxi and maxi-cab drivers who own and drive their own vehicle, with a valid licence and AP registration.

When will the 2026 Auto Drivers Sevalo money come?

As of 13 October 2026, no official date has been announced for the second year, though funds are reported as earmarked for 2026-27.

Can I apply if I drive someone else's auto?

According to the government's clarification, the scheme is only for drivers who own and personally drive their vehicle.

I didn't get the money last year. What can I do?

Contact your village or ward secretariat with your licence, RC and Aadhaar to have your eligibility checked.
