Telangana: ₹5 lakh family life insurance launched on 19 November

News reports say Chief Minister Revanth Reddy launched Indiramma Kutumba Jeevitha Bima at Siddipet on 19 November: a ₹5 lakh life cover for one earning member of each family (reported age 18 to 59), with the premium paid by the government. Reports say you need Aadhaar, ration card and mobile number, one application per ration card, and ration card e-KYC done at your ration shop. Reports differ on enrolment dates (door-to-door 20 to 30 November in one, month-long camps at gram panchayats and urban wards in another), so wait for the official notice from your Gram Panchayat or Tahsildar. Applying is free: never pay anyone. We have not found the official order, so details may change.
