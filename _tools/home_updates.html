Telangana: ₹5 lakh family life insurance to start on 19 November

The state Cabinet approved Indiramma Kutumba Jeevitha Bima, a ₹5 lakh life cover for the head or main earner of each family, with the premium paid by the government. It is to be launched on 19 November; Indiramma Committees are to collect applications house to house from 20 to 30 November. Full rules are not published yet. Applying is free: never pay anyone.
