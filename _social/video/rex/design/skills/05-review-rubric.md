# Skill 5: How the design expert reviews and approves

## Review procedure (every round)
1. Open the **full-size** PNG/JPG frames, not only contact sheets. Zoom into the faces.
2. List what you checked by file name. Never approve something you didn't open.
3. Score each category 1–5 (5 = broadcast-quality kids' TV). Anything below 4 needs a concrete fix.
4. For each fix, give **what is wrong, where (file + region), why it matters, and the exact change**. Use numbers where possible: "raise the eyes by 10 % of the head height", "tail lag of 6 frames".
5. Separate **blockers** (must fix before approval) from **polish** (nice to have).
6. Verdict line: `APPROVED`, or `CHANGES NEEDED (n blockers)`.

## Scoring categories
| Category | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Identity & appeal | Instantly a cute *baby T-Rex*, not a lizard, frog or dragon; you want to hug him. |
| Proportions & on-model | The head is 40–50 % of his height, and the landmarks are consistent in every view and pose. |
| Turnaround | Five views on shared guide lines with matching volumes; the views read as the same 3D creature. |
| Expressions | Each one reads in under half a second, all are distinct from each other, and head volume is kept. |
| Poses & silhouette | Clear line of action, and every pose reads as a black silhouette. |
| Motion (clips/strips) | Arcs, eased moves, follow-through on the tail, hat and arms, no sliding or floating. |
| Nature interaction | The body responds believably to wind, rain, cold, water and ground (Skill 4 table). |
| Atmosphere | Depth layers, light direction consistent with the shading on Rex, and prehistoric flora. |
| Style consistency | The pencil-storybook line quality matches the world; boil is subtle on the face; colour and B&W both work. |

## Constraints the expert must respect
- Everything is drawn by code (Canvas2D) in a pencil-sketch storybook style. No AI images, no downloaded assets. Suggest changes as shapes, numbers and rules that a programmer can implement.
- The audience is small children (Telugu and English). Keep him gentle and never scary, even the roar should be cute.
- Rex makes no speech; his sounds and expressions carry the story.
- Write review files only inside `_social/video/rex/design/reviews/`. Do not edit code.
