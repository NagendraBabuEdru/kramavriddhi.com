## Glowing drops (applies to 28–100)

The episode's title is "Twinkling Rain", and the drops currently look like normal blue rain.

**Make every glowing drop:**
- a round bead (not a teardrop) with a pale gold-white core, 12–16 px;
- a soft bloom: radial gradient, 40–60 px, additive at 35 %;
- a 4-point star sparkle rotating 15°/s and pulsing every 1.2 s;
- in B&W: a white core plus a white bloom on a slightly darker local sky, so the glow reads.

**Keep the two kinds of rain separate:**
- **ordinary rain** (from 90 s): thin diagonal streaks, never beads;
- **glowing drops:** slow beads, 40 % of the speed of ordinary rain.

## Top fixes, in priority order

1. **Make the river read as water** (145–218), in both versions.
   - Water colour: a lighter, warmer tan-ochre than the bank. Add a light highlight band near the top and a slightly darker band by the near bank.
   - 6–10 curved flow lines (white, 2 px, 50 %) scrolling downstream at different speeds.
   - White foam scribbles along both banks and around the raft and rocks.
   - Ripple rings around the raft.
   - The floating leaves drift *with the flow*, rotating slowly. Fewer of them (about 12), and only near the surface.
   - The bank needs a clear edge line plus a thin wet-dark strip where it meets the water.
   - **B&W:** water lighter than the bank with white flow lines, and the bank hatched darker.
2. **Raft on water, Rex on raft** (152–218).
   - Raft y = water-surface y at its x, plus a bob of ±8 px.
   - Draw a front water lip *over* the bottom 15 % of the raft so it sits in the water, not on it.
   - Rex's feet stay on the raft, the beetle stays on the raft or his hat, and the raft never touches grass after 151.5.
   - Remove the giant reflection at 152.
3. **Fix S20's "whee" drop** at 186: a river step, the raft over it, and Rex, the beetle and the hat floating above the raft.
4. **Glowing drops that glow** (see the section above), and visible rain streaks at 106–150.
5. **Weather and light continuity:**
   - darkest and wettest at 120–150, with the stream rising from 125;
   - rain lighter during the ride;
   - sun and clouds part only at 197;
   - no cross-hatch over the sky.
6. **Smaller fixes:**
   - Rex fully in frame at 55;
   - his face reflected in the puddle at 76;
   - rivulets at his feet at 135;
   - a big mist wall, shot from behind him, at 218;
   - knots off the letters and the footer added at 226.

## B&W or colour
**Colour again.** The bright green Rex, the red mushroom, the blue beetle and the rainbow are what this episode is about, and colour carries the warmth of the play scenes.

In B&W the river is currently grey on grey and unreadable, and the glowing drops are lost. After fixes 1 and 4, B&W could still look lovely in the rain scenes, which suit pencil (diagonal rain strokes over hatching).
