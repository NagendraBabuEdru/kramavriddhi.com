## Tiny last notes (do them if quick; none blocks the render)
1. **152:** the foam row of the water lip runs across the middle of the raft. Keep it on the raft's lower 15 % only.
2. **186:** the step's flat rock slabs look a little like floating shelves at three different heights. If it's quick: one row of rocks straight across the river, with a short white water-fall strip below it. Otherwise leave it, since it reads in motion.
3. **218:** let Rex's eyes catch the white mist glow (a bigger highlight), as planned for S22–S23.

## B&W or colour
**Colour is the main version.** The bright green Rex on warm ochre water, the gold twinkling drops, the blue beetle and the rainbow make this episode cheerful and clear for small children.

B&W now works too: the light water with white flow lines and the hatched bank read well at 175, and the rain strokes suit pencil. It is a good "storybook edition", but the twinkle and the rainbow, which are the heart of this episode, need colour.
