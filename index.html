Latest updates & deadlines

Don't miss important dates for scholarships, instalments and bookings.

News30 Oct 2026

PM-KISAN 24th instalment paid on 30 October

PIB says about ₹18,690 crore reached 9.34 crore farmers, ₹2,000 each. Not received? Check Know Your Status on pmkisan.gov.in (eKYC, land seeding, Aadhaar bank link). The 25th instalment date is not announced.
News31 Oct 2026

Annadata Sukhibhava 2nd instalment paid on 30 October

News reports say ₹7,000 per family (₹5,000 state + ₹2,000 PM-KISAN) was credited to about 46.86 lakh farmer families. About 17,828 farmers did not get it because of technical problems; ask at your Rythu Seva Kendram.
Expected2 Nov 2026

Thalliki Vandanam 2nd phase: ₹13,000 for new students delayed

The 2nd phase covers 3,29,201 students: new Class 1 and Inter first-year admissions and those left out in July. The order was issued on 10 October. The money was expected before Dasara but is not officially confirmed as paid by 2 November.
Deadline15 Oct 2026

AP crop insurance for Rabi: enrol by 15 December (paddy 31 December)

Under PM Fasal Bima Yojana, farmers pay at most 1.5% premium for Rabi food and oilseed crops. Enrol through your bank, CSC or Rythu Seva Kendram; an Agristack farmer ID is needed.

Indiramma Atmiya Bharosa (Telangana)

₹12,000 a year promised for landless farm labour families. Only part of the first instalment was paid in 2025; stalled since.

TelanganaFarmers & Agriculture

PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps for Farmers)

Up to 30% central subsidy for solar pumps, plus the state's share. Apply only through your state agency; beware fake websites.

Farmers & Agriculture

PM Vidyalaxmi Education Loan Scheme

Education loans with no collateral or guarantor for students in top colleges. 3% interest help if family income is up to ₹8 lakh.

Education & Skills

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Crop loans for farmers, fish farmers and livestock farmers at 7% interest, 4% if repaid on time. Up to ₹2 lakh without collateral.

Farmers & Agriculture

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (Rooftop Solar)

Up to ₹78,000 subsidy to put solar panels on your roof and get up to 300 units of power a month. Open till March 2027.

Housing

PM Ujjwala Yojana (Free LPG Connection)

Free LPG connection, first refill and stove for women from poor families. ₹300 subsidy now on 4 refills a year (from June 2026).

About Kramavriddhi

Kramavriddhi (क्रमवृद्धिः) is Sanskrit for "growth in steps." Our motto, क्रमेण वर्धामहे, means "we grow step by step." It's the idea behind everything we make: real progress comes from small, consistent steps.
