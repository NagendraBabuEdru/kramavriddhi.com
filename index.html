📅 Expected24 Oct 2026

PM-KISAN 24th instalment: 30 October reported

News reports on 23 October say the Agriculture Ministry announced 30 October 2026 on social media. No PIB press release yet. Make sure your eKYC, land seeding and Aadhaar bank link are done.
📅 Expected24 Oct 2026

Annadata Sukhibhava 2nd instalment: 30 October reported

It was not paid at Dasara. News reports say AP will pay it on 30 October together with PM-KISAN's 24th instalment. The date and amount are not officially confirmed yet. Complete your eKYC at the Rythu Seva Kendram.
📅 Expected24 Oct 2026

Thalliki Vandanam 2nd phase: ₹13,000 for new students delayed

The 2nd phase covers 3,29,201 students: new Class 1 and Inter first-year admissions and those left out in July. The order was issued on 10 October. The money was expected before Dasara but is not confirmed as paid by 24 October.
⏰ Deadline15 Oct 2026

AP crop insurance for Rabi: enrol by 15 December (paddy 31 December)

Under PM Fasal Bima Yojana, farmers pay at most 1.5% premium for Rabi food and oilseed crops. Enrol through your bank, CSC or Rythu Seva Kendram; an Agristack farmer ID is needed.

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Crop loans for farmers, fish farmers and livestock farmers at 7% interest, 4% if repaid on time. Up to ₹2 lakh without collateral.

About Kramavriddhi

Kramavriddhi (क्रमवृद्धिः) is Sanskrit for "growth in steps." Our motto, क्रमेण वर्धामहे, means "we grow step by step." It's the idea behind everything we make: real progress comes from small, consistent steps.
